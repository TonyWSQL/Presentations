The Long Way Home

Sep 20 – 22, 2026  ·  Gulf Coast Route, ~1,485 mi

KEY WEST → MIAMI → MACON → NASHVILLE → LAFAYETTE no escort, bike on trailer, home Tuesday

BEGIN TRANSACTION
Three days home. Bike gets one last ride — Key West to Miami on Sunday — and then it goes on the trailer for good. Monday is the long day: Alligator Alley, four Gulf Coast dealers, through Tampa and Gainesville without stopping, and a first Buc-ee's that doesn't show up until Warner Robins, Georgia. Florida has exactly zero of them on this route, which feels like a personal slight. Shot glass and poker chip at every stop; no challenge coin — that's for when you're actually riding, not towing. Tuesday is straight home with a #SQLFamily late lunch in Nashville, which is the right way to end a Florida trip.

Sunday · Sep 20
Back to Miami
~160 mi · 🏍️ Last ride · No H-D stops
🏍 Last morning in Key West. One more time down Duval — swing by Jimmy Buffett's Margaritaville Records studio while you're at it — fill the tank, head north on US-1. The Overseas Highway on a Sunday is about as good as it gets.
📍 Pigeon Key — MM 47. Pull off at Knight's Key and walk the old Seven Mile Bridge — cars aren't allowed on the original span, so it's foot traffic and turquoise water on both sides. Historic Flagler railway workers' island at the far end. 20 minutes.
📍 Whaling Wall #95 Mural — MM 52, Marathon. Giant marine mural on the side of the Marathon shopping center. Pull over, snap a photo, back on the bike. Five minutes.
🍽 Lunch in Marathon — Castaway Waterfront, Burdines, or Island Fish Co. Waterfront, shellfish, key lime pie. Midpoint of the ride.
📍 Islamorada — MM 81.5–82 (two-fer). Bass Pro Shops World Wide Sportsman has a sister ship of Hemingway's fishing boat the Pilar on display inside. Walk next door to Rain Barrel Artisan Village for Big Betsy the Lobster — a 30-foot giant lobster sculpture that is exactly what it sounds like. Park once, hit both.
📍 3D Great White Shark Mural — Key Largo. Roadside pull-off, quick photo. The Keys do not lack for wall art.
🏍 Hampton Inn Kendall by late afternoon — same hotel as Night 4 on the way down. Bike goes on the MC-10 trailer. That's it for two wheels until home.
⛽ Top off the Jetta Sunday evening — standard station near Kendall. No Buc-ee's in Florida on the return route; first one isn't until Warner Robins, GA (~480 miles away). Start Monday full.
Monday · Sep 21
Miami to Macon via Gulf Coast
~685 mi · 4 H-D stops · Jetta + trailer
🚗 9:00 AM, I-75 W into Alligator Alley. Tank is full from last night. The Alley is about 80 miles of wetlands and tollbooths with one service plaza (Miccosukee, MM 35) as a backup — fill before entering, not during.
🚗 Alligator Alley Harley-Davidson, Sunrise — I-75 / Sawgrass Expressway, right at open (Mon 10 AM). ~25 miles from the hotel, so the timing works cleanly. Shot glass, poker chip, back on the road.
⛽ Naples, FL — standard station at I-75 Exit 101. After crossing the Alley (~100 miles from Miami), this is the fuel stop before the Fort Myers and Port Charlotte dealers.
🚗 Rockstar Harley-Davidson, Fort Myers — 9501 Thunder Rd off I-75 Exit 131, ~25 miles past Naples. Shot glass and poker chip. Then another 35 miles up to Port Charlotte.
🚗 Bert's Black Widow Harley-Davidson, Port Charlotte — El Jobean Rd, open Mon 9–6. Adrenaline H-D Sarasota is closed Mondays; Tampa Bay H-D is also closed Mondays. Bert's is where the route goes instead, and it doesn't disappoint.
🚗 Sarasota and Tampa: I-75 N, no stops. Both dealers closed Monday. Through Tampa by early afternoon.
🚗 War Horse Harley-Davidson, Ocala — 5331 N US Hwy 441, off I-75 Exit 354. Open Mon 9–6, so the 3:30 PM arrival is safe. Last dealer of the return trip. Fuel here too before the push to Georgia.
⛽ Buc-ee's, Warner Robins GA — I-75 Exit 144. First Buc-ee's of the entire return. Florida gave us zero; Georgia delivers at mile ~480. 20 minutes, stretch, diesel, something from the fudge counter if discipline is low.
🏠 Hampton Inn & Suites Macon I-475 — same hotel as outbound Night 2. ~685 miles on the day. Check-in 4:00 PM.
Tuesday · Sep 22
Macon to Lafayette via Nashville
~640 mi · #SQLFamily lunch · Home
🚗 9:30 AM out of Macon. The math on Atlanta is simple: leave by 9:30, through the city by 11:15 AM, clear of both rush windows. Morning rush ends around 9; afternoon doesn't start until 3:30. This is the gap.
⛽ Buc-ee's, Adairsville GA — I-75 Exit 306, ~85 miles from Macon. Same one from Day 2 of the outbound. Fuel up before Atlanta.
🚗 Atlanta ~11:15 AM, Chattanooga by noon. I-24 W from Chattanooga toward Nashville — clocks fall back an hour at Monteagle (~90 miles in) crossing into the Central time zone.
🍽 Nashville — #SQLFamily late lunch with Jason & Sheila Romans (The Data Shepherd). ~3 PM CT. One of: Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint (Donelson, whole hog pit), Peg Leg Porker (The Gulch, dry-rub hickory), or Edley's Bar-B-Que (Donelson, Nashville institution). ~1 hour.
⛽ Buc-ee's, Smiths Grove KY — I-65 Exit 38, ~75 miles from Nashville. Same one from Day 1 of the trip out. Clocks forward CT→ET crossing into Indiana shortly after.
🏠 Lafayette by ~6:30 PM ET. ~1,485 miles, three days, four Gulf Coast dealers, one #SQLFamily lunch. COMMIT TRAN
