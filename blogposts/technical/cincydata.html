CincyDATA Is Back

Online meetups start November 18. In-person networking in Cincinnati is planned for early 2027.

The Cincinnati Data and AI Users Group (CincyDATA) is meeting again. I'm stepping in as interim organizer to get things moving, and I need help to make it work.

The group is for people who work with data and AI, on-premises and in the cloud, with a focus on Microsoft's data platform. It has been without an organizer for a while, and its Meetup page has been quiet since 2022.

Interim, On Purpose

I'm based in Indianapolis, not Cincinnati, so I'll be upfront about what this is. It's an interim arrangement. The goal is to keep the group alive, get meetings back on the calendar, and hand it to a Cincinnati-based organizer when the right person is ready.

Online meetups come first because they're the quickest way to get people back in the same room, so to speak. Once there's some momentum, we'll start meeting in person in Cincinnati. Details on that will follow as plans come together.
